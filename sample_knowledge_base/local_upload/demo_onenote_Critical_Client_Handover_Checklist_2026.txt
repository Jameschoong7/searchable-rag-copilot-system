Critical Client Handover Checklist 2026

Effective date: 1 August 2026

Use this checklist for P1 service incidents or any client handover after 6:00 PM MYT.

Required handover fields
- Incident owner
- Client impact
- Current status
- Next action and deadline
- Evidence or incident-record link

Timing
- Outgoing coordinator posts the handover in the Operations channel before 6:30 PM MYT.
- Incoming coordinator acknowledges the handover within 15 minutes.
- No acknowledgement after 15 minutes: escalate to the Operations Duty Manager.

Closure control
- Do not close the handover until the incident record contains the final action, owner, and timestamp.
- Verbal handover alone is not sufficient evidence.

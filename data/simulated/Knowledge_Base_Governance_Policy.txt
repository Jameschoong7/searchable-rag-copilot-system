CENTIFIC MALAYSIA — IT POLICY
Document: Knowledge Base Governance Policy
Version: 1.0 | Effective Date: 20 April 2026
Department: IT & Engineering
Classification: Internal Use Only

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. PURPOSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
This document defines the rules for creating, reviewing, approving,
publishing, and maintaining internal knowledge base documents at
Centific Malaysia.

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that employees can access
accurate, current, and approved information when using internal
documentation platforms, search tools, and AI-assisted knowledge
retrieval systems.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. SCOPE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
This policy applies to all internal knowledge base documents used by
Centific Malaysia employees, including but not limited to:

  - IT procedures
  - Engineering setup guides
  - Troubleshooting documents
  - Onboarding materials
  - Standard operating procedures
  - Internal FAQ documents
  - Security and access control guides

This policy applies to documents stored in approved company platforms
such as SharePoint, OneDrive for Business, approved GitHub repositories,
and company-managed knowledge base systems.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3. DOCUMENT OWNERSHIP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Every knowledge base document must have a clearly assigned document
owner.

The document owner is responsible for:

  - Ensuring the document is accurate.
  - Reviewing the document on schedule.
  - Updating outdated procedures.
  - Removing incorrect or duplicated content.
  - Confirming the correct classification label.
  - Approving the document before publication.

Documents without an assigned owner may be archived or removed from
active search results.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4. DOCUMENT APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Before a document is published to the internal knowledge base, it must
be reviewed and approved by the relevant department owner.

Approval is required for:

  - New documents
  - Major procedure changes
  - Security-related updates
  - Access control instructions
  - Client-impacting operational procedures
  - Documents used by AI search or chatbot systems

Draft documents must not be indexed into AI-assisted knowledge systems
unless clearly marked as draft and approved for testing.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
5. DOCUMENT QUALITY STANDARDS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Knowledge base documents must be written clearly and consistently.

Each document should include:

  - Document title
  - Version number
  - Effective date
  - Department owner
  - Classification label
  - Purpose section
  - Step-by-step procedures where applicable
  - Troubleshooting section where applicable
  - Review date

Documents should avoid unclear wording, outdated screenshots, broken
links, duplicated instructions, and unsupported assumptions.

Technical documents should include enough detail for another authorised
employee to repeat the procedure without depending on informal verbal
guidance.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
6. CLASSIFICATION AND ACCESS CONTROL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Each knowledge base document must use one of the approved classification
labels:

  - Public
  - Internal Use Only
  - Confidential
  - Restricted

Access permissions must match the classification level.

Internal Use Only documents may be accessible to general employees.
Confidential and Restricted documents must only be accessible to
authorised users based on role, department, or business need.

AI-assisted search tools must respect the same document permissions as
the original storage location. A user must not receive answers generated
from documents they are not authorised to view.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
7. REVIEW CYCLE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Knowledge base documents must be reviewed regularly to ensure accuracy.

The standard review cycle is:

  - IT procedures: every 6 months
  - Engineering guides: every 6 months
  - Security documents: every 3 months
  - HR and onboarding documents: every 12 months
  - Project-specific documents: at project closure or major milestone

If a document is found to be inaccurate before the scheduled review
date, the document owner must update or archive it immediately.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
8. OUTDATED OR DUPLICATED DOCUMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Outdated or duplicated documents must not remain active in the knowledge
base.

When duplicate documents exist, the document owner must:

  - Identify the most accurate version.
  - Merge useful content if needed.
  - Archive older versions.
  - Update internal links to point to the approved version.
  - Notify relevant users if a commonly used document has changed.

Archived documents must be clearly marked as archived and must not be
used as the source of official procedures.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
9. AI KNOWLEDGE RETRIEVAL USAGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Documents indexed into AI-assisted knowledge retrieval systems must be
approved, current, and properly classified.

The AI system must not generate answers from:

  - Expired documents
  - Draft documents without approval
  - Archived documents
  - Restricted documents without user permission
  - Personal notes or unofficial instructions

When the AI system cannot find approved information, it must respond
that the information is not available instead of guessing or generating
an unsupported answer.

All AI-generated answers must include source citations where possible.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
10. EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Employees are responsible for using the knowledge base correctly.

Employees must:

  - Refer to approved documents when performing company procedures.
  - Report outdated or incorrect documentation.
  - Avoid creating unofficial duplicate guides.
  - Avoid uploading confidential content into unauthorised systems.
  - Verify critical procedures against the original source document.
  - Follow document classification and sharing rules.

Employees must not rely only on informal chat messages or personal notes
when performing security-sensitive or client-impacting tasks.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
11. COMPLIANCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The IT Engineering Team may audit knowledge base documents to ensure
compliance with this policy.

Documents that fail governance checks may be removed from active search,
archived, or returned to the owner for correction.

Repeated failure to maintain accurate documentation may be escalated to
the relevant department manager.

CENTIFIC MALAYSIA - HUMAN RESOURCES POLICY
Document: Employee Leave Policy 2026
Version: 3.0 | Effective Date: 01 January 2026
Department: Human Resources
Classification: Internal - All Employees

1. PURPOSE
This policy explains annual leave, medical leave, emergency leave, and the
approval process for employees of Centific Malaysia.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE
Employees receive annual leave according to their employment contract.
Leave must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working days before
the intended leave date. The direct manager must approve the request before
the employee begins leave.

For leave periods longer than 5 consecutive working days, employees should
submit the request at least 14 calendar days in advance so project coverage
can be planned.

3. MEDICAL LEAVE
Employees must inform their manager as soon as possible when they are unable
to work due to illness. A medical certificate is required when medical leave
extends beyond 1 working day.

4. EMERGENCY LEAVE
Emergency leave may be requested when an unexpected personal situation makes
advance notice impossible. The employee should notify the manager and submit
the HR portal request within 2 working days after returning to work.

5. APPROVAL FLOW
1. Employee submits leave request in the HR portal.
2. Direct manager reviews project coverage.
3. Manager approves or rejects the request.
4. HR records the final leave balance.

Questions should be directed to hr-helpdesk@centific.com.

CENTIFIC MALAYSIA - HUMAN RESOURCES GUIDE
Document: Employee Expense Claim Submission Guide
Version: 2.2 | Effective Date: 10 February 2026
Department: Human Resources
Classification: Internal - All Employees

1. PURPOSE
This guide explains how employees submit reimbursement claims for approved
business expenses such as local travel, meals during client visits, and
approved office supplies.

2. REQUIRED INFORMATION
Each claim must include:
- claim category
- business purpose
- transaction date
- amount in Malaysian Ringgit
- receipt or invoice image
- approving manager

3. SUBMISSION DEADLINE
Claims must be submitted through the HR portal within 30 calendar days from
the transaction date. Claims submitted after the deadline require HR review
and may be rejected.

4. APPROVAL FLOW
1. Employee uploads receipt and claim details.
2. Direct manager validates the business purpose.
3. Finance reviews the amount and supporting document.
4. Approved reimbursement is included in the next available payroll cycle.

5. RESTRICTIONS
Personal expenses, unapproved subscriptions, and incomplete claims are not
reimbursable. Contact hr-helpdesk@centific.com for submission issues.

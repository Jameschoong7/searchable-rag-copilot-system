CENTIFIC MALAYSIA - SECURITY RUNBOOK
Document: Security Incident Reporting And Response Guide
Version: 2.5 | Effective Date: 01 February 2026
Department: Security
Classification: Internal - All Employees

1. PURPOSE
This guide explains how employees report suspected security incidents and
what information should be included.

2. REPORT IMMEDIATELY
Employees should immediately report:
- suspicious login notifications
- lost or stolen company devices
- suspected phishing emails
- accidental sharing of confidential files
- malware or ransomware warnings

3. REPORTING CHANNEL
Email security-operations@centific.com or call extension 1900. Include your
name, department, the affected system, the time observed, and a short
description. Do not forward suspicious attachments.

4. FIRST RESPONSE
Disconnect an affected device from the network if malware is suspected.
Do not delete files, reset the device, or attempt your own investigation.
Security staff will preserve evidence and provide next steps.

5. ESCALATION
Security Operations coordinates with IT, department leadership, and legal or
privacy stakeholders when required.

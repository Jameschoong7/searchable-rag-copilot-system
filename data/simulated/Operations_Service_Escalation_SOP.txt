CENTIFIC MALAYSIA - OPERATIONS SOP
Document: Internal Service Escalation Procedure
Version: 1.6 | Effective Date: 15 February 2026
Department: Operations
Classification: Internal - Operations And Project Managers

1. PURPOSE
This SOP defines how operational issues are escalated when an internal
service affects delivery, customer support, or employee productivity.

2. SEVERITY LEVELS
- Severity 1: service unavailable with major business impact
- Severity 2: degraded service affecting multiple users
- Severity 3: limited issue with workaround available

3. ESCALATION FLOW
1. Log the incident in the service desk portal.
2. Record affected users, service, time detected, and business impact.
3. Notify the service owner.
4. Escalate Severity 1 issues to the Operations Duty Manager immediately.
5. Provide status updates until service recovery is confirmed.

4. COMMUNICATION FREQUENCY
Severity 1 incidents require an update every 30 minutes. Severity 2 incidents
require an update every 2 hours until a workaround or resolution is available.

5. POST-INCIDENT REVIEW
A review should document root cause, corrective actions, and follow-up owners.

Version: 1.0 | Effective Date: 10 April 2026
Department: IT & Security
Classification: Internal Use Only

1. PURPOSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
This document defines how Centific Malaysia stores, retains, backs up,
and disposes of internal business data. The purpose of this policy is
to ensure that important company information remains available,
recoverable, and protected from accidental loss, unauthorised access,
or improper deletion.

All employees who create, modify, store, or manage company data must
follow this policy.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. DATA CLASSIFICATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Company data must be handled according to its classification level.

PUBLIC
  - Information approved for public release.
  - Examples: published blog posts, public website content, marketing
    material.

INTERNAL USE ONLY
  - Information intended for employees and authorised contractors.
  - Examples: internal procedures, onboarding documents, engineering
    guidelines.

CONFIDENTIAL
  - Sensitive business or client-related information.
  - Examples: client project documents, financial records, internal
    reports, system architecture documents.

RESTRICTED
  - Highly sensitive data with strict access controls.
  - Examples: credentials, security incident records, legal documents,
    privileged system access records.

Employees must not change the classification of a document unless they
are authorised by the document owner or department manager.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3. DATA RETENTION PERIODS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Centific Malaysia retains data based on business, legal, and operational
requirements.

GENERAL INTERNAL DOCUMENTS
  - Retention period: 3 years
  - Examples: internal guides, SOP drafts, meeting notes.

ENGINEERING DOCUMENTATION
  - Retention period: 5 years
  - Examples: architecture diagrams, deployment notes, API documents,
    troubleshooting guides.

HR AND EMPLOYEE RECORDS
  - Retention period: 7 years or according to applicable legal
    requirements.
  - Examples: employment records, leave records, expense claim records.

SECURITY LOGS
  - Retention period: 1 year minimum
  - Examples: VPN access logs, authentication logs, endpoint security
    alerts.

PROJECT DOCUMENTS
  - Retention period: Based on client agreement or project closure
    requirements.
  - Confidential client documents must not be retained beyond the
    approved retention period.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4. BACKUP REQUIREMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Critical company data must be backed up regularly to prevent data loss.

The following backup schedule applies:

  - Daily backup for active project repositories and shared workspaces.
  - Weekly backup for internal knowledge base documents.
  - Monthly backup for archived business records.
  - Immediate backup after major system configuration changes.

Backups must be stored in approved company-managed storage locations
only. Employees must not create personal backup copies on USB drives,
personal laptops, or personal cloud storage accounts.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
5. APPROVED STORAGE LOCATIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Company data must be stored only in approved platforms.

Approved storage locations include:

  - Microsoft SharePoint
  - Microsoft OneDrive for Business
  - Azure Blob Storage
  - Approved GitHub organisation repositories
  - Company-managed internal file servers

The following storage locations are not allowed for company data:

  - Personal Google Drive
  - Personal Dropbox
  - Personal iCloud
  - Personal USB drives
  - Personal email accounts
  - Unapproved external file-sharing websites

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
6. DATA RESTORATION PROCEDURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
If data is accidentally deleted, corrupted, or overwritten, employees
must submit a restoration request through the IT Helpdesk portal.

The request must include:

  - File or folder name
  - Original storage location
  - Approximate date and time of deletion or corruption
  - Business reason for restoration
  - Department manager approval if the data is confidential or restricted

IT will review and process restoration requests based on backup
availability and data classification.

Standard restoration requests are typically processed within 2 business
days. Urgent business-critical requests may be escalated by the
department manager.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
7. DATA DISPOSAL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Data that has exceeded its retention period must be securely deleted or
archived according to company procedures.

Employees must not manually delete confidential or restricted data
unless instructed by the document owner, department manager, or IT
Security Team.

For restricted data, deletion must be verified by IT Security to ensure
that no unauthorised copies remain in shared folders, local devices, or
backup locations.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
8. EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Employees are responsible for handling company data properly.

Employees must:

  - Store work files in approved company platforms.
  - Avoid keeping duplicate local copies unnecessarily.
  - Report missing, corrupted, or accidentally deleted files promptly.
  - Follow document classification labels.
  - Avoid sharing files with unauthorised users.
  - Delete temporary working files when they are no longer needed.

Failure to follow this policy may result in loss of access privileges or
disciplinary action.

9. REVIEW AND COMPLIANCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The IT Security Team may conduct periodic audits to verify that company
data is stored, retained, backed up, and disposed of correctly.

Departments are responsible for reviewing their own documents and
informing IT when outdated or expired information should be archived or
removed.

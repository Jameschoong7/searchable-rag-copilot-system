CENTIFIC MALAYSIA - SECURITY POLICY
Document: Access Control And Role Assignment Policy
Version: 3.1 | Effective Date: 20 January 2026
Department: Security
Classification: Restricted - Security And IT Administrators

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines how employees receive access to internal systems and how
role assignments are reviewed.

2. PRINCIPLE OF LEAST PRIVILEGE
Employees receive only the permissions required for their current job
responsibilities. Access must not be granted solely for convenience.

3. ACCESS REQUEST FLOW
1. Employee submits an access request with business justification.
2. Direct manager validates the business need.
3. System administrator assigns the approved role.
4. Security team reviews the audit log.

4. REVIEW FREQUENCY
Privileged access must be reviewed every 90 days. Access that is no longer
required must be removed promptly.

5. RESTRICTIONS
Employees must not share credentials or approve their own privileged-access
requests. Contact security-operations@centific.com for urgent access issues.
